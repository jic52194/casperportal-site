Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the CasperPortal Partner Program.

1. What is the earning potential as a CasperPortal affiliate?

As a CasperPortal affiliate, your earning potential is significant. You earn $150 per month for every business you successfully enroll in our CRM program. These earnings continue as long as the business remains active in the program. For example, if you enroll 10 businesses, you would make $1,500 per month. The more businesses you sign up, the more you can earn!

2. How does the cookie life work for affiliate tracking?

The cookie life for affiliate tracking is 365 days. This means that if a user clicks on your affiliate link and signs up within 365 days of their first visit, you will be credited with the sale and earn your commission.

3. What are the payout terms for affiliates?

CasperPortal operates on a Net-15 payout schedule. This means that you will receive your payout on the 15th of the month following the month in which earnings were generated. Payments are sent via PayPal, providing a secure and convenient method to receive your earnings.

4. What are my responsibilities as an affiliate?

Your primary responsibility as an affiliate is to promote CasperPortal's CRM platform to potential clients. You should use honest and ethical methods, adhering to all applicable laws and regulations. You are permitted to use the marketing materials provided by CasperPortal but are not authorized to modify them without prior written consent.

5. Are there any prohibited activities?

Yes, certain activities are prohibited to maintain the integrity of the affiliate program. These include: Spamming (sending unsolicited emails or messages). Using adware, spyware, or malware. Misleading advertising or making false claims about CasperPortal's services. Offering unauthorized incentives (e.g., cash back, rewards) to potential clients. Affiliates must not bid on keywords containing CasperPortal's trademarked terms in any search engine advertising programs (e.g., Google Ads).

6. How does CasperPortal handle intellectual property?

CasperPortal grants affiliates a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use CasperPortal's trademarks, logos, and marketing materials solely for the purpose of promoting the CRM program. Affiliates must not modify the provided marketing materials in any way without prior written approval from CasperPortal. All intellectual property rights remain with CasperPortal.

7. What happens if I want to terminate my affiliate relationship?

Either party may terminate the affiliate relationship at any time, for any reason, with 30 days' written notice to the other party. Upon termination, you will no longer be entitled to receive any commissions for new sales generated after the termination date. However, you will continue to receive commissions for existing clients you enrolled before termination as long as those clients remain in the program.

8. What legal protections are in place for affiliates?

CasperPortal provides several legal protections for affiliates, including: Limitation of Liability: CasperPortal will not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from your participation in the Affiliate Program. Indemnification: Affiliates agree to indemnify and hold harmless CasperPortal from any claims arising from their promotional activities. Force Majeure: CasperPortal will not be liable for any delays or failures to perform due to circumstances beyond its control.
