How the program works

  1. Apply and get approved. You must be at least 18 and approved by CasperPortal to become an affiliate.
  2. Share your referral link. Introduce CasperPortal to businesses that need a CRM with built-in messaging, automation, booking and reviews.
  3. Earn monthly. For every business you enroll in the $297 per month plan, you earn $150 per month as long as the business remains active.

Example only: if you enrolled 10 businesses and all 10 stayed active, your commission would be 10 × $150 = $1,500 per month. Actual earnings depend entirely on the businesses you enroll and keep.

Frequently asked questions

How much do CasperPortal affiliates earn?

You earn $150 per month for every business you enroll in the $297 per month plan, for as long as that business remains active in the program.

How long does the referral cookie last?

The cookie lasts 365 days. If someone clicks your link and signs up within 365 days of their first visit, the sale is credited to you.

When are commissions paid?

Payouts are Net-15: earnings for a month are paid on the 15th of the following month. For example, January earnings are paid on February 15.

What am I not allowed to do as an affiliate?

Spam, adware or malware, misleading claims, unauthorized incentives and bidding on CasperPortal trademarked terms in search ads are prohibited. You must also disclose your affiliate relationship in your promotions.

What happens to my commissions if I leave the program?

Either party may end the relationship with 30 days' written notice. You stop earning on new sales after termination but continue to earn on businesses you enrolled before termination while they remain in the program.

The full rules are in the CasperPortal affiliate terms.
