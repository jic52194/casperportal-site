How to Stop Missing Calls and Losing Leads

Every missed call is a missed opportunity. Learn how to implement a missed-call text-back system to instantly engage prospects before they call your competitors.

For: Local service business owners

By Jared Casper · April 4, 2026 · 5 min read

Why This Matters

You're missing 20–40% of inbound calls during busy hours. The moment that call goes to voicemail, 62% of callers will hang up and call the next business on Google (BrightLocal, 2024).

The Real Cost of a Missed Call

For a local HVAC company, a single new customer is worth $300–$1,200 in first-year revenue. If your phone rings 20 times a day and you miss 6 of those calls, and even 2 of those 6 would have booked — that's $600–$2,400 per day in lost potential revenue.

Most service businesses don't track missed calls because they're not visible. You only see what came in. You never see what walked away.

Run the Math on Your Business

Here's a conservative calculation for a local plumbing company:

  • Daily inbound calls: 15
  • Miss rate during busy hours: 30% = 4–5 missed calls/day
  • Conversion rate↗ if called back within 5 minutes: ~40%
  • Average job value: $350
  • Missed revenue per day: 2 jobs × $350 = $700
  • Missed revenue per month: ~$14,000

Now ask yourself: if you could recover even 1 extra job per day by sending an instant text when you miss a call, what would that be worth in a year?

Here is a tip: A plumber missing 2 jobs per day at $350 each = $8,400/month in unrecovered revenue.

What Happens When You Miss a Call Without a System

Without automation, here's what happens:

1. Your phone rings. You're on a job, driving, or with a client. 2. The call goes to voicemail. 3. The caller hangs up — 62% don't leave a voicemail (BrightLocal, 2024). 4. They open Google and call the next business. 5. You call back 2 hours later. They've already booked someone else.

With missed-call text-back↗ , what happens instead:

1. Your phone rings. You miss it. 2. Within 30 seconds, the caller receives a text: *"Hey, this is [Business Name] — sorry we missed you! What can we help you with today?"* 3. They reply. You're now in a conversation while they're still deciding. 4. You book the job from your phone before you're even back to your truck.

  1. Call comes in, you don't answer
  2. CasperPortal triggers an instant SMS to the caller
  3. Caller replies — lead is now captured
  4. Conversation continues in your CasperPortal inbox
  5. You book the job and sync it to your calendar

What Staying Unautomated Will Cost You This Month

Every day you don't have missed-call text-back↗ running is a day you're giving leads to your competitors. For a business averaging 15 calls/day with a 30% miss rate:

  • 5 missed calls/day × 22 working days = 110 missed calls/month
  • If 25% would have booked = 27 lost jobs
  • At $350 average = $9,450 in unrecovered revenue per month

This isn't about being perfect. It's about making sure every lead you worked to generate actually gets a response.
