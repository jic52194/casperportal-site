How to Stop Missing Business Calls (and the Leads Behind Them)

By Jared Casper, Founder · April 4, 2026 · 6 min read

Here's how to stop missing business calls: set up a system that responds when you can't pick up. Route every call through one business number, send an automatic text to anyone you miss, and keep those replies in one inbox you can answer from your phone. That way a missed call becomes a conversation instead of a lost lead.

Why missed calls cost more than you think

Most service businesses don't track missed calls because they aren't visible. You see the jobs that came in. You never see the callers who hung up, opened Google, and called the next business on the list.

When you're on a roof, under a sink, or with a client, you can't answer. That's normal. The problem isn't missing the call — it's having nothing in place that responds for you.

Run the math on your own business

You don't need an industry average to see the stakes. Use your own numbers:

  1. Count how many calls you miss on a typical day (your phone's call log shows this).
  2. Estimate how many of those callers would have booked if someone had responded quickly.
  3. Multiply by your average job value.

For example, if you miss 6 calls a day, 2 of those callers would have booked, and your average job is worth $350 to you, that's $700 of potential work walking away each day. Your numbers will be different — the point is to put a real figure on a problem you can't otherwise see. The ROI calculator can run this for you.

What happens when you miss a call without a system

  1. Your phone rings while you're on a job, driving, or with a client.
  2. The call goes to voicemail.
  3. The caller hangs up without leaving a message.
  4. They search again and call the next business.
  5. You call back hours later. They've already booked someone else.

Four steps to stop missing business calls

1. Put every call on one business number

You can keep your existing business number by porting it into CasperPortal, or use a local tracking number that forwards to your phone. Either way, every call is logged in one place, so you can see who called and who you missed. Call tracking also shows which marketing source made the phone ring.

2. Turn on an automatic text for unanswered calls

Missed-call text-back sends a text you write to anyone whose call goes unanswered — no manual step. Your message can include:

  • Your business name, so they know who's texting
  • A direct question ("What can we help with?") that invites a reply
  • A booking link, if you use online booking
  • A note about when you'll call back

3. Answer every reply from one inbox

Texts, emails and social messages all come into the same unified inbox, attached to the contact's record. You can reply from the iOS or Android app between jobs, or hand the conversation to someone in the office.

4. Follow up with anyone who doesn't reply

Some callers won't answer the first text. A simple workflow can send one more message the next day and create a task for you to call them back, so nobody slips through.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to stop missing business calls?

Turn on an automatic text that goes to every caller you don't answer, and send those replies into one inbox you can check from your phone. You still miss the call, but the caller gets a response and a way to keep the conversation going.

Can I keep my current business phone number?

Yes. With CasperPortal you can port your existing business number or use a local tracking number that forwards to your phone.

What should a missed-call text say?

Keep it short: your business name, an apology for missing the call, and a question that invites a reply, such as "What can we help you with today?" You can add a booking link if you take appointments online.

Do I need a receptionist to stop missing calls?

Not necessarily. An automatic text-back plus a shared inbox covers the most common case — a caller who just needs a quick response — and you can still call people back personally when the job needs it.
