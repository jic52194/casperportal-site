What changes from one industry to the next

The software is the same for everyone. What changes is how it is configured:

  • Pipeline stages that match your sales steps, such as inquiry, estimate sent, booked and won.
  • Booking rules, reminders and deposits that fit how customers schedule with you.
  • Follow-up automations and message templates written for your customers.
  • Review requests sent when a job or visit is marked complete.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a different version of CasperPortal for each industry?

No. Every business gets the same full platform on the Growth Plan. The industry pages show how to configure it for your type of business.

My industry is not listed. Can I still use CasperPortal?

Yes. The pipelines, automations and booking tools are fully customizable, so any business that captures leads and follows up with customers can use it. Book a demo to talk through your setup.
