CRM ROI Calculator

This CRM ROI calculator estimates what missed-call text-back and tool consolidation could be worth to your business. Enter your monthly leads, missed-call rate, close rate, average sale value and current software costs, and it estimates extra monthly sales, revenue and savings against CasperPortal's $297 per month Growth Plan.

How the calculation works

  1. Missed calls. Monthly leads multiplied by your missed-call rate gives the number of leads that reach voicemail.
  2. Recovered leads. The calculator assumes 40 percent of those missed callers are recovered by an automatic text-back. This is a planning assumption built into the tool, not an industry average or a measured result. Your own recovery rate may be higher or lower.
  3. Extra sales and revenue. Recovered leads multiplied by your close rate gives extra sales per month, and extra sales multiplied by your average sale value gives the monthly revenue gain.
  4. Tool savings. If your current tools cost more than $297 per month, the difference is added as savings. The annual figure is the monthly total multiplied by twelve.

All results are estimates based on the numbers you enter and the recovery assumption above. Actual results depend on your industry, your follow-up and how quickly you respond.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the recovery rate in the calculator come from?

It is an assumption built into the calculator so it can produce an estimate. It is not an average, a benchmark or a guarantee, and your own results may differ.

What should I enter for missed-call rate?

Use the share of your inbound calls that go to voicemail or are not answered. Your phone provider's call log, or a week of tracking, will give you a realistic figure.

Does the calculator include the cost of CasperPortal?

Yes. Tool savings are calculated against the $297 per month Growth Plan. SMS usage is billed separately and is not included.

Is my data saved?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and the numbers you enter are not submitted anywhere.
