About This Topic

Local SEO is one of the highest-leverage investments a North Shore small business can make — and one of the most misunderstood. It's not about gaming Google. It's about consistently sending the right signals: an optimised Google Business Profile, consistent NAP data across directories, steady review velocity, and locally-targeted content on your website.

This hub covers every local SEO tactic, framework, and audit checklist we've published for businesses in Georgetown, Haverhill, Newburyport, and the surrounding North Shore communities. Whether you're starting from zero or trying to climb from position 4 to position 1 in the Map Pack, these guides are built for local service businesses — not national brands with enterprise SEO budgets.
