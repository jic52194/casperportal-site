Security & Trust

Platform Security

We treat your customer data like it's our own. CasperPortal is built on a foundation of proactive security practices to keep your business safe.

Data Encryption

Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard encryption protocols.

Secure Infrastructure

Hosted on modern cloud infrastructure designed for high availability, reliability, and scale.

Access Controls

Granular user permissions ensure your team only sees the data and tools they need to do their jobs.

System Monitoring

Proactive security practices, system monitoring, and regular updates to protect against vulnerabilities.

Data Backup & Retention

We maintain regular system backups to ensure platform continuity in the event of hardware or infrastructure failure. However, customers are responsible for maintaining their own independent backups of customer data, contacts, and configurations. Data retention follows our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Incident Response

We maintain internal protocols for identifying, assessing, and responding to potential security incidents. In the event of a verified data breach affecting your account, we will notify affected account owners in accordance with applicable legal requirements and our Terms of Service.

Reporting Security Issues

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in CasperPortal, please contact us immediately. We investigate all credible reports.
