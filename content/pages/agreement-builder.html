Frequently asked questions

What is in the agreement packet?

The packet includes client information, the selected plan and billing terms, payment authorization, selected and declined services, and signature pages.

How do I save or send the packet?

Generating the packet opens your browser's print dialog, where you can print it or save it as a PDF to attach to an email.

Who provides the optional services in the packet?

CasperPortal provides the software. Done-for-you setup and other professional services are provided by CasperB2B and are included only if selected in the packet.
