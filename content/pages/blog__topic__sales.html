About This Topic

Most local businesses don't have a lead problem — they have a follow-up problem. Leads come in, get two touches, and disappear. Meanwhile, 80% of sales happen after five or more contacts (Marketing Donut). The gap between those two numbers is where revenue goes to die.

This hub covers every sales system, sequence, and strategy we've documented for local and B2B service businesses. From 7-day follow-up cadences to pipeline management and closing frameworks, each guide is built with specific copy, timing, and automation notes — not just general advice. Implement one article this week and you'll outpace most competitors who are still following up by hand.
