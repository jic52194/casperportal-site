About CasperPortal

CasperPortal is a CRM and marketing automation platform for local service businesses, based in Georgetown, Massachusetts. It puts customer communication, follow-up, and next steps in one place, so missed calls, delayed replies, and scattered notes stop costing you customers. It was founded by Jared Casper (Founder) and Mark Casper (Co-Founder).

  • Conversations stay organized in one place
  • Follow-up happens automatically so leads don't slip
  • Owners and managers can see what happened and what needs to happen next

How We Think

Four pillars that keep the platform grounded in how local businesses actually work.

01 · Who we serve

Teams where every lead and reply matters. CasperPortal fits call- and appointment-driven businesses that want one system to keep customer work clean and consistent. See industries we serve.

  • Local operators juggling phones, forms, and inboxes
  • Teams that need one shared view of customer history
  • Owners who want fewer tools and fewer handoffs

02 · What the platform does

Centralize communication and make follow-up consistent. Instead of stitching tools together, the platform keeps leads, conversations, and next steps organized so the team stays aligned. See all features.

  • One record per customer with full context
  • A structured timeline of messages and activity
  • Simple automation for confirmations and reminders

03 · Implementation approach

Start from your current reality, then refine. Some teams move off spreadsheets; others migrate from another CRM. Either way, the work is to map how leads arrive today and configure the system to fit.

  • Map inquiry sources and handoffs
  • Configure stages, fields, and permissions
  • Train teams on simple, repeatable steps

04 · Operator-led principles

Clarity, accountability, and fewer moving parts. We build for real people serving real customers. If a feature adds complexity without a clear payoff, it doesn't belong.

  • Make the next step obvious
  • Keep ownership clear across the team
  • Reduce tool sprawl and duplicated work

Common Questions

Is CasperPortal built for small local teams?

Yes. It's built for busy operators who need one place to manage inquiries, follow-up, and next steps without extra complexity.

What problems does the platform solve first?

Speed-to-response, consistent follow-up, and clear pipeline visibility. Those three remove the biggest sources of lost opportunities.

Where is CasperPortal based?

CasperPortal is based in Georgetown, Massachusetts, and serves local businesses across the North Shore, Essex County, and the Merrimack Valley.

How does CasperB2B relate to CasperPortal?

CasperB2B is a separate agency that offers optional done-for-you setup of CasperPortal for businesses that want to skip the learning curve. Using CasperPortal does not require it.
