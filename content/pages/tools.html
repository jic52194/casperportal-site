Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use the tools?

No. The ROI calculator, quiz and agreement builder run in your browser without signing up.

Are the ROI calculator results guaranteed?

No. The calculator uses the numbers you enter plus a stated recovery assumption, so treat the result as an estimate for your own planning.

How do I save an agreement packet?

The agreement builder opens your browser's print dialog, where you can print the packet or save it as a PDF.
