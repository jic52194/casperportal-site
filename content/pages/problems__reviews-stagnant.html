How do I get more Google reviews for my business?

The most effective way to get more reviews is to ask for them immediately after a positive experience. Using an automated reputation management tool, you can trigger an SMS and email request the moment a job is marked complete in your CRM.

Why are consistent reviews important for local SEO?

Google's algorithm favors businesses with a steady velocity of new reviews. A business with fifty 5-star reviews from this month will often outrank a business with two hundred 5-star reviews from three years ago.

How do you handle negative reviews?

A good reputation management system directs happy customers to public review sites (like Google or Facebook) and routes unhappy customers to a private feedback form, giving you a chance to resolve the issue before it damages your public rating.
