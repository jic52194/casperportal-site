Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is CasperPortal?

CasperPortal is CRM and marketing automation software built for local service businesses. It replaces your separate CRM, email marketing tool, funnel builder and scheduling software with one platform where all the data is connected.

How does the missed-call text-back work?

When a lead calls and you can't answer, CasperPortal detects the missed call and sends your own text message to the caller, so they stay with you instead of calling a competitor. Their reply lands in your inbox.

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Yes. You can port your existing number into CasperPortal, or use a local number that forwards to your current phone while enabling the automation features.

How much does CasperPortal cost, and is there a contract?

The self-serve trial is $97/month after 14 days. The Growth Plan is $297/month, or $2,970/year (save 17%), with unlimited users and unlimited contacts. There is no setup fee and no long-term contract. Self-serve plans are month-to-month, and each starts with a 14-day free trial.

Do you offer done-for-you setup?

Yes, as an option. If you'd rather not build it yourself, CasperB2B, a separate agency, offers done-for-you CasperPortal setup. See done-for-you setup for what's included.
