Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with CasperPortal.

What exactly is CasperPortal?

CasperPortal is an all-in-one business operating system designed specifically for local service businesses. It replaces your CRM, email marketing tool, funnel builder, and scheduling software with one unified platform that actually talks to each other.

How does the missed-call text-back work?

When a lead calls your business and you can't answer, CasperPortal instantly detects the missed call and sends an automated, personalized text message to the caller. This keeps them engaged and prevents them from calling your competitor while you're busy.

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Yes. We can either port your existing number into the platform or set up a local tracking number that forwards directly to your current phone while enabling all of our automation features.

Do you offer 'Done-For-You' setup?

Absolutely. Most of our clients choose our DFY path where the CasperB2B agency team handles your full implementation, lead migration, and automation build-out so you can just focus on running your business.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. We believe in earning your business every month. Our self-serve plans are month-to-month, and our DFY plans focus on long-term growth without locking you into restrictive multi-year agreements.
