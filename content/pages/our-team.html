Meet the CasperPortal Team

The CasperPortal team is led by Founder Jared Casper and Co-Founder Mark Casper, two operators from sales and auto dealership backgrounds who built the CRM they needed. The company is based in Georgetown, Massachusetts, and builds practical systems that close deals and keep customers cared for.

Founder

Jared's path ran from flea markets and direct sales to managing high-volume auto sales floors and building several businesses — each of which hit the same problem: leads lost to fragmented software and weak follow-up.

  • One place for leads, conversations, and accountability
