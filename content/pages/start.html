Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free trial for CasperPortal?

Yes. CasperPortal offers a 14-day free trial of the Growth Plan so you can use the full CRM and automation platform before you pay.

What is included in the CasperPortal subscription?

The Growth Plan includes full CRM access, missed-call text-back, automated email and SMS follow-up, a unified team inbox, calendar booking, pipeline tracking, and reputation management, with unlimited users and unlimited contacts.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your plan begins automatically at $297/month and is billed through Stripe. If you cancel during the 14-day trial, you are not charged.

Is there a setup fee or contract?

No. The self-serve plan has no setup fee and no long-term contract; it is month-to-month.

Can I keep my business phone number?

Yes. You can port your existing number into CasperPortal, or use a local tracking number that forwards to your current phone.
