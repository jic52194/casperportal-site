On average, a missed call costs a local service business between $250 and $1,500 in lost revenue, depending on the industry. When a potential customer reaches voicemail, 80% will hang up and call the next competitor on Google.

What is missed call text-back automation?

Missed call text-back is an automated system that instantly sends a text message (SMS) to anyone whose call you miss. For example, it might say: 'Hi, this is [Business Name]. We're on the other line—how can we help you today?' This keeps the lead engaged and prevents them from calling a competitor.

Can I use my existing business phone number for missed call text-back?

Yes. CasperPortal can either port your existing business number or set up a tracking number that forwards directly to your main line, allowing the system to detect missed calls and fire the automated text response seamlessly.
