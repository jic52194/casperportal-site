The Ultimate Guide to B2B Follow-Ups

Discover the exact follow-up sequences and multi-channel strategies we use to convert cold leads into booked appointments without being annoying.

For: B2B service providers, agencies, consultants, and local businesses who generate leads but struggle to convert them into scheduled calls or appointments.

By Jared Casper · March 28, 2026 · 8 min read

Why This Matters

80% of sales require 5 or more follow-up touchpoints. Most salespeople give up after 2 (Marketing Donut). Your leads aren't cold — your follow-up is.

Why Your Follow-Up Isn't Working

The #1 reason leads don't convert isn't price, fit, or timing — it's that nobody followed up consistently.

Here's what typically happens: - Day 1: You send an intro email. - Day 3: No reply. You feel awkward. You move on. - Day 30: That lead signs with a competitor who followed up 7 times.

The data is brutal: 80% of non-routine sales happen after at least 5 contacts, but 44% of salespeople give up after just one follow-up (Marketing Donut / Invesp). The gap between those two numbers is where your revenue is disappearing.

The problem isn't your product. It's that following up 5+ times manually feels pushy, gets forgotten, and doesn't scale.

The 7-Touch Follow-Up System That Doesn't Feel Spammy

The goal isn't volume — it's sequenced multi-channel contact that adds value at each touchpoint↗ . Here's the cadence we use:

  • Day 1: Intro email
  • Day 2: SMS check-in
  • Day 4: Value email (case study, tip, or relevant resource)
  • Day 6: Voicemail drop
  • Day 8: Final value SMS
  • Day 10: "Last touch" email with a clear soft close
  • Day 14: Breakup email — creates urgency and often generates a reply

This sequence spans two weeks, touches all three channels (email, SMS, phone), and gives every lead a fair chance to convert.

Exact Messages — Copy and Use These

The breakup email (Day 14) consistently generates the most replies in the entire sequence. Something about finality triggers people to respond. Use it.

1Day 1 — Intro EmailSubject: "Quick question about [Company Name]"

Hey [First Name], I came across [Company] and noticed [specific observation]. We help [target outcome] — would it make sense to chat for 15 minutes this week? [Your Name]

2Day 2 — SMS

Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] from [Company]. Sent you an email yesterday — just making sure it didn't land in spam. Happy to answer any questions by text. 👍

3Day 4 — Value EmailSubject: "How [Similar Business] got [specific result]"

Hey [First Name], thought this might be useful: [short case study or tip specific to their industry]. Let me know if you'd like to see how we'd apply this to [Company Name].

4Day 6 — Voicemail Drop

Hey [First Name], [Your Name] here. Left you a couple messages — I know you're busy. I'll be brief: we've helped businesses like yours [specific result]. If that sounds interesting, shoot me a text or hit reply on my email. Hope to connect soon.

5Day 8 — SMS Check-In

Hey [First Name] — any chance you had a moment to look at my emails? No pressure at all, just wanted to make sure you got them.

6Day 10 — Soft Close EmailSubject: "Still interested?"

Hey [First Name], I've reached out a few times and want to be respectful of your time. If now isn't the right moment, completely understood — just let me know and I'll follow up in a few months. If you are still interested, here's a link to grab 15 minutes: [Calendar Link]

7Day 14 — Breakup EmailSubject: "Closing your file"

Hey [First Name], I'll assume the timing isn't right and won't bother you again. If things change and you want to explore [specific offer], feel free to reply anytime. Wishing you a great [quarter/year]. — [Your Name]
