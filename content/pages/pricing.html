Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Self-Serve and Done-For-You?

Self-serve gives you full access to the platform to build your own workflows. Done-For-You means the CasperB2B agency team builds your entire system for you, from funnels to automations.

Can I switch from Self-Serve to DFY later?

Yes, you can upgrade to a DFY implementation at any time if you decide you want our expert team to take over the technical heavy lifting.

Is there a setup fee for the Self-Serve plan?

No. Our self-serve software plans have zero setup fees. You only pay the monthly subscription.

What happens after the 14-day free trial?

Your selected plan will automatically begin. You can cancel at any time during the trial to avoid being charged.
