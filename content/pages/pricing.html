Frequently Asked Questions

How much does CasperPortal cost?

The CasperPortal Growth Plan is $297/month, or $2,970/year (about $247.50/month, a 17% saving). It includes unlimited users and unlimited contacts.

What is the difference between self-serve and done-for-you?

Self-serve gives you full access to the platform to build your own workflows. Done-for-you means CasperB2B, a separate agency, builds your system for you, from funnels to automations.

Is there a setup fee or a long-term contract for the self-serve plan?

No. The self-serve plan has no setup fee and no long-term contract. It is month-to-month; you only pay the subscription.

What happens after the 14-day free trial?

Your selected plan begins automatically and you are billed through Stripe. You can cancel at any time during the trial to avoid being charged.

Can I switch from self-serve to done-for-you later?

Yes. You can add a done-for-you implementation later if you decide you want CasperB2B to handle the technical setup.
