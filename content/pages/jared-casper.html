Why it exists

Built from frustration, not theory

CasperPortal didn't start as a "let's build software" idea. It started as the same problem showing up everywhere: leads coming in from different places, conversations split across inboxes, and follow-up depending on someone remembering.

The Beginning

Flea Markets & Direct Sales

Started by learning the raw fundamentals of human attention, trust, and crafting offers that actually work in face-to-face environments.

The Grind

Auto Sales & Dealership Leadership

Managed high-volume sales floors where speed to lead and clear communication were the only things that separated winning months from losing ones.

The Pivot

Building Multiple Ventures

Scaled several businesses, realizing that every single one suffered from the same problem: dropping leads due to fragmented software and poor follow-up systems.

Today

Founding CasperPortal

Built the operator-first CRM designed specifically to capture demand, standardize follow-up, and stop businesses from losing money to inefficiency.

What it does

One place for leads, messages, and next steps

CasperPortal centralizes customer work so teams move faster, follow up consistently, and stop losing opportunities in the gaps.

Capture leads from forms, pages, chat, calls, and social

Keep email and SMS organized in a single timeline

Use clear pipelines so ownership and status are obvious

Automate confirmations, reminders, and basic nurture

Monitor reviews and visibility signals that drive trust

Report on activity and outcomes without spreadsheet chaos
