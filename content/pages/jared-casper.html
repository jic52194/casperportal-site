Why It Exists

Built from frustration, not theory

CasperPortal didn't start as a "let's build software" idea. It started as the same problem showing up everywhere: leads coming in from different places, conversations split across inboxes, and follow-up depending on someone remembering.

  1. The beginning · Flea markets and direct sales

    Started by learning the raw fundamentals of human attention, trust, and crafting offers that work in face-to-face environments.

  2. The grind · Auto sales and dealership leadership

    Managed high-volume sales floors where speed to lead and clear communication separated winning months from losing ones.

  3. The pivot · Building multiple ventures

    Built several businesses and realized every one suffered from the same problem: dropped leads due to fragmented software and poor follow-up systems.

  4. Today · Founding CasperPortal

    Built the operator-first CRM designed to capture demand, standardize follow-up, and stop businesses from losing money to inefficiency.

What CasperPortal Does

One place for leads, messages, and next steps

CasperPortal centralizes customer work so teams move faster, follow up consistently, and stop losing opportunities in the gaps.

  • Capture leads from forms, pages, chat, calls, and social
  • Keep email and SMS organized in a single timeline
  • Use clear pipelines so ownership and status are obvious
  • Automate confirmations, reminders, and basic nurture
  • Monitor reviews and the signals that build trust
  • Report on activity and outcomes without spreadsheet chaos
