Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Text To Pay.

Is text-to-pay secure?

Yes. CasperPortal uses industry-standard encryption and partners with secure processors like Stripe to ensure every transaction is safe for both you and your customer.

What is the fee for text-to-pay?

CasperPortal does not charge additional transaction fees. You only pay the standard processing fees from your connected payment provider (e.g., Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢).
