Frequently Asked Questions

What is CasperPortal exactly?

CasperPortal is an all-in-one business management and growth platform. It combines CRM, marketing automation, email/SMS marketing, appointment booking, and reputation management into a single dashboard designed specifically for local service businesses.

How does missed-call text-back work?

When a customer calls your business number and you don't answer, CasperPortal instantly detects the missed call and sends an automated text message (e.g., 'Sorry we missed your call! How can we help?'). This keeps the lead engaged before they call a competitor.

Can I migrate my data from another CRM?

Yes! We support CSV imports for contacts and can help with manual migration from platforms like HubSpot, Salesforce, or spreadsheets during your onboarding process.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No. All self-serve plans are month-to-month and you can cancel at any time. Our Done-For-You plans have a setup fee but no long-term lock-in contracts.
