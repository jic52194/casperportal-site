Frequently asked questions

What is CasperPortal?

CasperPortal is an all-in-one platform for local service businesses that combines CRM, marketing automation, email and SMS marketing, appointment booking and reputation management in one dashboard.

How does missed-call text-back work?

When a customer calls your business number and you do not answer, CasperPortal detects the missed call and sends an automated text, such as "Sorry we missed your call. How can we help?", so the conversation keeps going.

Can I migrate my data from another CRM?

Yes. You can import contacts by CSV, and the team can help with migration from platforms like HubSpot, Salesforce or spreadsheets during onboarding.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No. The self-serve plan is month-to-month with no setup fee. The 14-day free trial is billed after it ends, and you can cancel anytime during the trial.

Can I keep my current business phone number?

Yes. You can port your existing business number or use a local tracking number that forwards to your phone.
