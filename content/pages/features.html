How the features work together

Capture

Leads arrive from forms, website chat, Facebook Messenger and phone calls, and each one becomes a contact record in the CRM.

Respond

Every conversation lands in one unified inbox, and missed-call text-back replies to callers you could not answer.

Follow up

Workflow automations and triggers send the next text or email, book the appointment and move the deal along your pipeline.

Get paid and reviewed

Send invoices or text-to-pay links, then ask happy customers for a review with reputation management.

Frequently asked questions

Are all features included in one plan?

Yes. The Growth Plan is $297 per month with unlimited users and unlimited contacts, or $2,970 per year. You can try it free for 14 days.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. CasperPortal runs in the browser and has iOS and Android apps for replying to leads, managing your calendar and sending invoices on the go.

Can someone set the features up for me?

Yes. CasperB2B offers optional done-for-you setup of the platform. See the setup options or the pricing page for details.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. The self-serve plan is month-to-month with no setup fee, and you can cancel anytime during the free trial.
