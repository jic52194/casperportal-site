The Casper Journey

Built for Operators.

We didn't just build a software platform. We built the solution we desperately needed when running our own operations. This is how CasperPortal became the backbone of modern business growth.

2020

Identifying the Problem

We noticed local businesses were losing millions due to missed calls and disorganized follow-ups. The seed for CasperPortal was planted.

2021

The First Prototype

Built the initial MVP focusing purely on speed-to-lead and unified messaging. Tested it with a small group of beta users.

2022

Early Adopters & Iteration

Expanded our user base. Learned that operators needed more than just messaging—they needed automated pipelines and review management.

2023

Enterprise Scale

Rebuilt the architecture to support Fortune 500 level security and scalability. Introduced advanced AI-driven automations.

2024

CasperPortal Today

The premier enterprise-grade CRM and growth platform, helping thousands of businesses organize leads and scale efficiently.
