Why This Matters

You see software as an expense rather than an investment. You don't know if your tools are actually making you money.

The Simple CRM ROI Formula

ROI isn't complicated. It's (Gains - Costs) / Costs. For a CRM, your gains come from recovered leads and saved time, while your costs are the monthly subscription and setup fees.

Most businesses focus only on the cost. They see $297/month. They don't see the $2,000/month in leads they're losing because they don't have a system.

Lead Recovery: The Most Direct Path to ROI

If you miss 10 calls a month and recover just 2 of them using missed-call text-back↗ , and your average job is $500, that's $1,000 in 'found' revenue. That one feature alone pays for CasperPortal 3 times over.
