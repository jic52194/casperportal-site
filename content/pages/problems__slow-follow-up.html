Over 50% of sales happen after the 5th follow-up, yet 44% of salespeople give up after just one attempt. Consistent follow-up builds trust, stays top-of-mind, and drastically increases the chance of converting a lead into a paying customer.

How do you automate lead follow-up?

You can automate lead follow-up using a CRM like CasperPortal. When a lead enters your system (via form, call, or chat), it triggers a multi-channel sequence (SMS, email, voicemail drops) spaced out over days or weeks, ensuring no lead falls through the cracks.

What is the best follow-up cadence for service businesses?

A proven cadence is: Day 1 (Immediate text & email), Day 2 (Value-add email), Day 3 (Check-in text), Day 5 (Voicemail drop), and Day 7 (Final offer or break-up email). Automation handles this entirely in the background.
