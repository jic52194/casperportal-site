Frequently asked questions

Do I need integrations for texting, email and booking?

No. SMS, email, booking, reviews and the unified inbox are built into CasperPortal, so integrations are only needed for tools outside those jobs.

What if a tool I use is not listed?

Zapier and webhooks can connect CasperPortal to many other apps. Contact us or book a demo to check a specific tool.

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Yes. You can port your existing number or use a local tracking number that forwards to your phone.
